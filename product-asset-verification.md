# Product asset and CTA verification

The storefront preview displays the original seeded products plus eleven user-provided image products. The product grid shows explicit `Add to Cart +` and `Shop Now →` controls for every in-stock product. Browser interaction on the first Add to Cart button changed the bag counter from 0 to 1 and showed the toast `Dew Ritual Hydrating Serum added to your bag`. Shop Now is wired to add the selected product and open the bag drawer.
